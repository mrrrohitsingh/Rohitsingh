ROHIT SINGH WEBSITE - FREE LAUNCH STEPS

1. Open index.html in Notepad. Find CFG (near the bottom) and paste your
   email, Telegram link, Instagram link and LinkedIn link between the quotes.
2. Also replace the About placeholders in [brackets] and the photo box.
3. Free hosting (pick one):
   A) Netlify Drop: go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag this whole folder. Done, you get a free https link.
   B) GitHub Pages: create a free GitHub account, make a public repository,
      upload these files, then Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch (main).
4. After you get your link, replace YOUR-SITE-URL in robots.txt and sitemap.xml
   with it (without https://), and re-upload.
5. Google Search Console (free): add your site, verify, then Sitemaps > submit sitemap.xml.
   Use URL Inspection > Request indexing. Indexing takes time and rankings are not guaranteed.
6. Free hosting can change its limits later. Keep a backup copy of these files.
